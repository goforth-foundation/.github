# GoForth Foundation

**Empowering Good, Everywhere.**

GoForth Foundation is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization that builds innovative tools and platforms to empower nonprofits, religious organizations, and communities — helping them simplify operations, amplify outreach, and foster meaningful connections in their mission to do good.

## Our Story

Originally founded as the private charity *Doing Good Foundation*, GoForth Foundation grew into a public charity dedicated to bridging the gap between vision and execution. We're driven by faith, community, and the power of technology to create lasting impact.

## Mission

Guiding God's children on their journey to Him through community empowerment — uplifting communities through resources, classes, events, and broadcasting, while building reverence for temples worldwide.

## Our Projects

This organization maintains a portfolio of platforms and software tools, including:

- **[Temples.org](https://temples.org)** — A central hub for temple information and resources
- **[TempleTours.org](https://templetours.org)**
- **[MesaTemple.org](https://mesatemple.org)**
- **[TempleHill.org](https://templehill.org)**
- **[DCTempleVisitorsCenter.org](https://dctemplevisitorscenter.org)**
- **SaintsOfJesusChrist.org**
- **Wikipedia temple updates** — Ongoing contributions to keep temple-related articles accurate and current
- **Invite, Coordinate, Share, and Report** — A suite of software tools for organizational operations and outreach
- Regional sites for Las Vegas, Arizona, and North Texas

## Why We Build

1. **Innovative Solutions** — Cutting-edge tools that help organizations streamline operations and extend their reach.
2. **Global Reach** — Our platforms serve Latter-day Saints worldwide, across languages, locations, and devices.
3. **Personalized Support** — We work closely with partners to tailor solutions to their unique missions.
4. **Faith-Driven Mission** — Grounded in faith and committed to uplifting communities doing good.
